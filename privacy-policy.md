Privacy Policy for Move2Wake
Effective Date: September 30, 2026
Last Updated: September 30, 2026
Move2Wake (“the App”) is an alarm application designed to help users wake up by requiring them to complete a selected number of squats. This Privacy Policy explains how information and device permissions are handled when you use Move2Wake.
1. Information We Collect
Move2Wake is designed to operate primarily on your device.
The current version of Move2Wake does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.
Alarm information and preferences, including alarm times, labels, selected repeat days, enabled/disabled status, and selected squat targets, are stored locally on the user's device.
2. Camera Access
Move2Wake uses the device camera to detect body movement and count squats when an alarm is active.
Camera access is used solely for providing the squat-detection functionality of the App.
Move2Wake does not intentionally record, save, upload, or transmit photographs or videos captured by the camera. Camera frames are processed for pose and movement detection to determine whether a squat has been completed.
Camera permission must be granted for camera-based squat detection to function.
3. Alarm and Notification Permissions
Move2Wake may request permissions necessary to provide its alarm functionality, including permissions for:
- scheduling alarms;
- displaying notifications;
- displaying alarm-related content when appropriate;
- waking or keeping the device screen active while an alarm is being handled; and
- restoring applicable alarms following a device restart.
These permissions are used to provide the alarm functionality requested by the user.
4. Local Storage
Move2Wake stores alarm settings and related preferences locally on the device.
This information may include:
- alarm time;
- alarm label;
- selected recurring days;
- number of squats required; and
- whether an alarm is enabled.
This locally stored information is used to operate the App and is not intentionally transmitted to Move2Wake servers.
Deleting the App may also delete locally stored application data, subject to the device's operating system, backup, and restore behavior.
5. Squat and Pose Processing
Move2Wake analyzes body-position information derived from camera frames to detect squat movements.
This processing is used to determine whether the user has completed the required number of squats for an active alarm.
The App is not intended to identify individuals through facial recognition or biometric identification.
6. Third-Party Technology
Move2Wake uses Android platform technologies and may use third-party software libraries necessary to provide functionality such as camera access and pose detection.
The current implementation uses Google's ML Kit technology for pose detection.
Third-party components may be governed by their respective privacy practices and terms. Move2Wake does not use these components for advertising or intentional user profiling.
Before each public release, the application's included dependencies should be reviewed to ensure that this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosures accurately reflect the released version.
7. Advertising and Analytics
The current version of Move2Wake does not intentionally include advertising, behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics functionality.
If these practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.
8. Children's Privacy
Move2Wake is a general-purpose alarm application and is not designed to collect personal information from children.
The App does not intentionally request personal information such as a user's name, email address, or precise location as part of its core functionality.
If future versions introduce accounts, cloud services, analytics, advertising, or other features involving additional data processing, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
9. Health and Physical Activity
Move2Wake's squat feature involves physical activity. It is provided as an alarm-completion feature and is not medical, health, or fitness advice.
Users should use the feature only when they can safely perform the selected activity. Users should stop using the physical-activity feature if they experience discomfort or believe that performing the activity may be unsafe for them.
10. Data Sharing and Sale
Move2Wake does not intentionally sell users' personal information.
Based on the current implementation, Move2Wake does not operate a backend service to which users' alarm settings, camera recordings, or squat counts are intentionally uploaded.
11. Security
Move2Wake is designed to minimize data collection by keeping its core alarm information on the user's device.
No application or device environment can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but reasonable efforts are made to limit the information processed to what is necessary for the App's functionality.
12. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated when Move2Wake's functionality, permissions, third-party technologies, or data practices change.
Any updated policy will display a revised “Last Updated” date.
